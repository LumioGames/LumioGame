## Residence, expiry boundary, death, Unknown and Clear

The actual Runtime settles all initial requests before finite period/control/expiry work. `FiniteEffectSettlement.CanInsert` counts current resident plus nonresident rows and bytes, including rows whose EndTick has arrived but whose expiration has not yet been retired. Then Remove controls run and expiration retires all resident rows with `world.Tick >= EndTick`. This is why region peak insertion space is `P*min(A,D+1)`, not `P*D`, in a normally resident, advancing world. Post-F ordinary live region rows are at most `P*min(A,D)`; recurring DueRecords and the generic two result credits still use configured L.

The draft03 owner checks `L≥3P+P*min(A,D+1)`. Current A110/D20/P8 gives192. The `3P` player baseline is the current authored/configured overlap allowance, **not an independent certificate for every legal timing overlay**. Bubble/Aura are mutually exclusive character families within a match, but source eligibility excludes active same-type rows using EndTick>Tick, not physical row presence. Freeze/immunity/removal and a new active-skill boundary need exact coexistence proof. Reducing PerTargetRows to1 or inferring storage loops from P=8 is invalid. Any new target-row limit must cover boundary insertions and must be checked against every supported duration/cooldown overlay and actual control ordering.

Each Favorite lease reserves its **entire** reachable duration A in the existing FireZoneCapacity before the first side effect. `CanReserve` refuses an existing same-Life lease, >P leases, duration0 or >Int32.MaxValue, whole-duration+historical-region credits beyond FireZoneCapacity, and a complete entity reserve beyond `World.EffectiveMaxLiveEntitiesBudget`. Current whole Favorite cohort is at most8*110=880 owed regions, even when all expired entities are retained. This is an entity/debt bound, not a claim that880 finite GAS rows stay alive in an advancing resident world. Unknown hold after expiry does not extend Runtime EndTick, produce more periodic effects, or mint a new open lease.

The owner writes Submitted witnesses before Create/Apply/Destroy. Uncertain submission is not retried. Parent known rejection can retire genuinely rejected children; parent/source death closes the lease and stops future emissions, while actual born regions retain full original tuple. `TryRetire` requires no pending ordinal, exact zone census equal to emitted ordinals, genuine expiry/native terminal phases, and no FireFacts/selected exposure/terrain/ice source hold. Retirement-submitted remains charged until actual absence. Unknown debt may persist without a timer; it must not be mistaken for free capacity or converted into catch-up emission. The fixed16KiB canonical JSON lease field is another admission constraint, checked by encoding all candidate leases before mutation; Root must measure worst actual eight full identity records and max timing digits.

RoundTransition's draft `Prepare` first refuses while Favorite leases or unconsumed FireFacts/other terrain and ice debt exist. It consequently does not blanket-remove10112 on source death or next-match reset. After these owners clear, it removes every actual finite row and waits. Existing Config C=7P describes one freeze cleanup plus two passes over a 3P baseline. **C=7P is not automatically sufficient after player-row expansion, nonresident restoration, or new control producers.** Source-certified conservative resident control count is `C_offer ≤ N_freeze_rows_seen_by_Consume + 2*N_finite_rows_seen_by_RoundPrepare`; Favorite rows are excluded from RoundPrepare by HasPending in the proposed bundle. Every successful repeated Remove consumes a control/result even if a later duplicate becomes Obsolete; count calls, not distinct handles.

Nonresident finite rows remain charged in actual Runtime insertion/startup/partition checks; global scalar clock advance alone does not prove that all stored rows have been physically retired. A complete compact L must either prove this Game world remains resident/pinned for the lease cadence and expiry work, or provision the retained nonresident cohort. Whole-region entity/debt credits bound regional obligations even under such delay, but do not certify the normal192-row estimate. Paired restore must consume actual remapped Runtime handles; active-world partial restore remains explicitly rejected per ADR0046. Root's lifecycle matrix must cover first business Tick expiration and source-oldLife holds under actual partition behavior.

`EffectQueue.Drain` copies every admitted snapshot into Retained, clears pending slots and marks Settled. Admission is then late-refused. Each reducer borrows a separate phase9 batch; at World result-window disposal the Runtime expires leases, clears Results/Retained/Controls/ResultFacts and releases fact scratch. Snapshot bytes, fact certificates and retired recurring result credits cannot be recycled during earlier reducers. Durable Game fact rows clear only in the ordinary consumer.
