## Favorite implementation constraints and open schema boundary

Spec inputs are design §7.6/8.4, stage0 kernel and ADR0046/0047. Favorite is snapshotted at accepted cast and carried by actual Aura parameters/full handle; later slot exchange does not rewrite that cast. While the actual Aura is active on that Life, its open 3×3 coverage leaves fire for 1000ms. Death stops new Aura emissions; previously emitted fire persists to its own actual lifetime. Aura, Burn and trail share one continuous exposure clock and stable source ordering. Trail pulses retain full original source and target-life fact identities, including after owner death. A trail may melt actual ice through the sole terrain transaction owner; it never explodes bombs or destroys blocks/pickups by a second path.

Existing FireZoneState has 13 persisted fields, CoverageMask, transform and native HFSM state, but **FireZoneEntity has no EffectComponent** and there is no finite trail lifetime Effect. Native FireZone kind7 / Active7001→Expired7002 / FireElapsed7101 / ExpireFire7101 exists; a private producer must still use real native lifecycle and GAS duration. Existing 10111 is player Aura with a player-specific settlement contract and cannot be silently reused as a FireZone lifetime effect. Any added Effect declaration/ID, EffectComponent entity membership, complete finite-handle persistence and generated storage/settlement cost requires an explicit Game schema proposal and official GEN. No new ID is assigned here, and no hand-written deadline substitutes for GAS expiration.

Current exposure enumerates only Burn and Aura. Its dormant Kind3 FireZone source validation is not a ZoneSources producer. Add an actual-zone source enumerator with full owner/source life/generation/match/chain/skill/family identity and actual bounded coverage; do not treat it as terrain material truth.
